Privacy Policy

ChronoCure

Effective Date: June 18, 2026

1. Introduction

ChronoCure ("the App") respects and protects the personal privacy of all users. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information while you use the App.

Important: The App is a fully offline application. It does not collect, upload, or share any personal user data.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information, including but not limited to:

  • Identity or contact details such as name, phone number, email address, or home address
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or geolocation data
  • Usage behavior data, crash logs, or analytics data
  • Sensitive permission data such as contacts, photos, microphone, or camera

All data you enter in the App — medication names, dosages, schedule times, check-in records, as well as diseases, checkup plans, and checkup records — is stored only in your device's local storage (UserDefaults). Checkup photos are kept in the App's private sandbox directory and are excluded from iCloud/iTunes device backups.

The App offers an optional iCloud backup and restore feature: when you are signed in to iCloud and use it, the data above together with your checkup photos is stored as a backup package in your own iCloud Drive. The backup is carried and transmitted by Apple's iCloud service, is visible only to you, and cannot be accessed, retrieved, or read by the developer in any way. "Export Data" is a system share action initiated by you, and you decide where the exported content goes.

Premium subscription purchases, trials, and renewals are processed entirely by the Apple App Store. The App never accesses or stores your payment information; we only learn from the App Store whether your subscription is active.

3. How Information Is Used

Because the App does not collect any user data, there is no scenario in which data is used for the following or any other purposes:

  • User profiling, advertising, or commercial promotion
  • Selling or sharing data with third parties
  • Cross-app or cross-device tracking

The App only uses the local notification service provided by iOS to send you medication and checkup reminders at the times you set. Notification content is configured entirely on your device and never leaves it.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, social, or cloud service SDKs, and it does not call any external network APIs. All features run locally on your device; the optional iCloud backup is transmitted by Apple's system-level iCloud service, which is not a third-party service, and the developer operates no servers of its own.

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by the iOS system sandbox, so other apps cannot access it directly. However, please note:

  • Except for the iCloud backup you choose to use (stored in your own iCloud account and inaccessible to the developer), data is stored only on your current device and is not backed up to any other cloud;
  • Deleting a disease or checkup plan permanently removes its checkup records and photo files from the device;
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored data;
  • Please keep your device secure to prevent unauthorized access by others.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not contain any content or services directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Policy Updates

Updated versions will be published on this page, and the "Effective Date" shown at the top of the page will apply. We recommend checking this page periodically to stay informed about the latest privacy practices.

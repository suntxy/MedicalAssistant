FAQ

Why am I not receiving medication reminders?
Please check the following: ① Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Medication Assistant and make sure Allow Notifications is turned on; ② You have added a medication in the app and set reminder times; ③ Do Not Disturb / Focus mode or Screen Time notification limits are not blocking alerts. The system requests notification permission on first launch; if you previously denied it, you need to re-enable it in system Settings.
Will my data be lost? Can I back it up?
All data is currently stored locally on your device in UserDefaults, and it is removed if you delete the app. We recommend taking screenshots of important medication records regularly. iCloud sync and local export are being considered for future versions.
How do I add or edit a medication?
Tap the "+" button at the bottom right of the home screen, then fill in the medication name, dosage, schedule times, and duration. Tap an existing medication to open its detail page, where you can edit its information or pause/resume its reminders.
Does the app require an internet connection?
No. Medication Assistant is a fully offline app. It does not collect or upload any personal data — everything stays on your device.
Which iOS versions are supported?
The current version supports iOS 16.0 and later. For the best experience, we recommend iOS 17 or newer.

Troubleshooting

If you encounter the following issues, try these steps:

  1. App crashes or stutters: Fully close the app from the app switcher and reopen it, or restart your device.
  2. Reminder times are inaccurate: Check that your system time zone and date & time settings are correct, then re-open the medication detail page to confirm the reminder times.
  3. Check-in records not showing: Pull down on the home screen to refresh; if the issue persists, check whether duplicate check-ins on the same day are being filtered.
  4. Notifications are silent: Make sure the device is not in silent mode and the notification volume is turned up.
If none of the above solves your problem, please contact us via the email above and include your device model, iOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue, so we can locate it quickly.
